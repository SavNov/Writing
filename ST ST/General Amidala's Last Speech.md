# General Amidala's Last Speech
"I did it. Because I've come to realize what many in the New Republic have come to realize: that the Senate is responsible for this cold war. That we've lost our way so completely, we've become the villains in this conflict. That **we** should be put on trial—all of us. My attack on the Naboo Senate was an attack on what the New Republic has become: a government so afraid of becoming the Empire, it would rather do nothing than risk doing the wrong thing. We've let the ghost of Palpatine haunt us so deeply that the Empire endures stronger in our hearts than it ever did in the Alderaan Killer. This New Republic is failing! And it won't be the First Order's army that topples it. It'll be welcomed with shields down, with complacency… and to some, thunderous applause. It will not be on the battlefield where Hux will defeat us, but in our own homes!"
