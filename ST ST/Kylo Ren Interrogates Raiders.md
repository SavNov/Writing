EXT. — FIRST ORDER OUTPOST — DORAMAN, NIGHT
A group of RAIDERS in ragged, dirty clothes set fire to a barracks. An alarm goes off. FIRST ORDER SOLDIERS evacuate the building, unarmed but in uniform.

Camera pans into shot of raiders from behind the shoulder of POE DAMERON, who turns away from a GROUP of FIRST ORDER PILOTS to see the raiders approach.

Without warning, the five raiders drop to the ground moaning in pain as if being crushed. Camera pans forward, showing from Poe's POV.

KYLO REN, 20s, black hair, Caucasian, tall and lean, wears black robes but carries no lightsaber, eyes staring forward in cold efficiency as his hand lays open at his side.

KYLO REN
(cold, detached, clinical)
Where is the location of your base?

RAIDER 1
(shaken, but determined)
I won't tell you nothin'. You don't scare me, monk.

Kylo sighs, and Raider 1 suddenly clutches his head screaming.

KYLO
There are two options here. Either I obtain the information I need from you, or I break your mind trying.

RAIDER 2
(horrified and angry)
He's not even armed! 
Kylo had been called to Doraman, site of a First Order base where new recruits would be trained. Raiders had set fire to a building. He was closer than the nearest troop. A waste of his time, but he was efficient.

With a flick of his fingers, he used the Force to crush the five men against the cold dirt. Running after them would take time.

"Where is your base, raider?" He asked, every trace of emotion vanished from his voice.
