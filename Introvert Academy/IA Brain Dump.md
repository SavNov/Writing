Emma is a sociopath and a brilliant profiler. She has an incredible ability to read people, know the human mind, especially the criminal mind, but is a prodigy of pharmacology when it comes to poisons and knows the human body inside and out. May or may not be a former serial killer. Her entry exam was to solve a 5-years-unsolved murder in 48 hours, and she succeeded. She is also the reason that Izzy despises profilers.
Christopher is an incredibly fast mind feeling trapped and limited by a human body. He started as a Chess prodigy, got bored of that after he'd mastered it and now works on Go. He loves probability and chance and games like Poker because he can't fully predict the outcome through number crunching. He's constantly finding backdoors and firewalls. His entry exam was to identify an undiscovered backdoor into a classified CIA database within 48 hours, which he passed. He comes off as abrasive and rude because his mind works so quickly that he often struggles to put his own thoughs into words, rushing ahead and leaping past numerous steps of logic he finds obvious, leaving others confused and him frustrated when he has to slow down further to help others understand.
Jamie is a perfectionist at heart. She can't stop herself. She's a prodigy painter and artist, always painting and drawing and sketching and frustrated when she sees something, knows it's off or wrong somehow, but can't figure out why. She relies on rules and discipline to keep her mind at ease. There is no one in the world better at spotting forgeries and fakes than she, but at the same time, her mind has never worked in words. A drawing will always hold more meaning to her than a novel, and that often means her sentences are carefully crafted and precise, because it takes a lot of effort to reach ones that sound right.
Alex is a prodigy of writing and the written word. A brilliant writer with a love of roleplay, strategy, RPGs and diplomatic codewords that may decide the fate of nations. He knows English, Spanish, Russian and Dutch, with varying degrees of fluency, always seeking the right word for the job. He's more social and dynamic. Unsure what his entry exam was.
And then there's Izzy. The extroverted, fluid, lithe spokesperson of a secret academy that even governments rarely ever see the footwork of. Ten black belts, working on her eleventh. And yet also...kind of the outcast. She's, ironically, the extrovert of Introvert Academy. Her physical skills are top notch, and she's the deadliest person in any room, yet rarely gets to *do* anything. And with her father as The Director, she's also worried. Her father is becoming increasingly withdrawn, shrinking away from the world, but also...getting scared? Something bad is coming, and she's not sure what, but she has this terrible feeling that their latest recruit may have brought just as much danger as he does talent. After all, it was her vast physical ability that inspired him to even found the Academy at all, that curiosity and wonder, of just...are there any others like her with such exceptional gifts? If those gifts could be allowed to grow and flourish...they could change the world. Like, really change the world, for the better.
Each room is tailor-fitted to the person, down to the books on the shelves...or lack of them. Jamie has a world of white walls full of potential, long tables for holding racks of art, dozens of notebooks filled with sketches. She's borderline OCD in her room design. She packs a ruler into her dressing room---because yes, she has a dressing *room*, not just a dresser---to make sure her dresses are exactly three-fourths of an inch apart. Her sheets are placed on the bed exactly the same way each time, because she checks, every single time. Alex has books. Wall-to-wall bookshelves full of tomes in English, Spanish, Russian and Dutch, with Arabic sanskrit scattered across walls. Chest after chest of D&D guides. And four TVs tuned into FOX News, CNN, Reuters, and BBC 1 at all times, always watching for codes in the language. Christopher's room is full of tablets, some with active games of Go, others Poker, others Chess, with Ergonomic keyboards and typing tutors at the ready so maybe his fingers will be able to finally work at the same rate as his racing mind. AHD, full blown. He's actively offended by QWERTY. At minimum, he insists on DVORAK. As each part of the usual laptops annoyed him, he was forced to ditch Windows altogether and go Ubuntu, then Fedora, then finally rattling down on Arch. BSPWM. Home row mods. And then, there's Aidan's, which he soon realizes is absolutely *perfect*.
