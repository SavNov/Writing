He comes off as abrasive and rude because his mind works so quickly that he often struggles to put his own thoughs into words, rushing ahead and leaping past numerous steps of logic he finds obvious, leaving others confused and him frustrated when he has to slow down further to help others understand.
Jamie is a perfectionist at heart. She can't stop herself. She's a prodigy painter and artist, always painting and drawing and sketching and frustrated when she sees something, knows it's off or wrong somehow, but can't figure out why. She relies on rules and discipline to keep her mind at ease. There is no one in the world better at spotting forgeries and fakes than she, but at the same time, her mind has never worked in words. A drawing will always hold more meaning to her than a novel, and that often means her sentences are carefully crafted and precise, because it takes a lot of effort to reach ones that sound right.
Alex is a prodigy of writing and the written word. A brilliant writer with a love of roleplay, strategy, RPGs and diplomatic codewords that may decide the fate of nations. And then there's Izzy. The extroverted, fluid, lithe spokesperson of a secret academy that even governments rarely ever see the footwork of. Ten black belts, working on her eleventh. And yet also...kind of the outcast. She's, ironically, the extrovert of Introvert Academy. Her physical skills are top notch, and she's the deadliest person in any room, yet rarely gets to *do* anything. And with her father as The Director, she's also worried. Her father is becoming increasingly withdrawn, shrinking away from the world, but also...getting scared? After all, it was her vast physical ability that inspired him to even found the Academy at all, that curiosity and wonder, of just...are there any others like her with such exceptional gifts? If those gifts could be allowed to grow and flourish...they could change the world. Like, really change the world, for the better.
Each room is tailor-fitted to the person, down to the books on the shelves...or lack of them.
