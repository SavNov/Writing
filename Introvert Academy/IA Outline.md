# Act I — Lethal Genius

## Welcome To My Life. I'm Rotting.

- Aidan Travis is an introverted loner with no social life, aware that his lifestyle is unhealthy but unsure what to do about it.
- He's a brilliant profiler with the ability to get into other people's heads, choosing to spend his time watching interviews and getting inside the head of a Middle Eastern cult leader named Khalid. Who remembers Khalid has friends, and he doesn't.

## You Have To Use Your Gifts To Help People, Or You Waste Them

- Aidan has agreed to go skateboarding with his friends, knowing he's the third wheel, but hoping against hope that he might be able to make friends, connect.
- His parents don't understand half of what he says or does, but they love him anyway. They're concerned that he has no real peers. They hope he'll make some real friends this time. He doesn't make false promises, just says what he knows. That he hopes he makes some real friends here too.
- We get our theme: You have to use your gifts to help people, or you waste them.

## My Life Sucks, But Izzy Is Different

- He heads to the skatepark, and we meet The Academy before we even meet The Academy. Izzy is extremely athletic, a prodigy athlete, yet is clearly improvizing every step even as she pulls off moves that would not be possible for even Olympic gold medalists. She is kind to him, and though he only realizes it due to another character's mention of it, incredibly attractive. All he understands is that Izzy has weapons in her parka, and that they only slightly less deadly than she is.
- Izzy shows him the ropes of skateboarding, getting one on one with him and isolating him from his friend group in the process. She helps him enjoy himself. Yet she says absolutely nothing about herself. Any attempts at engaging with her, learning PII, bounce off harmlessly.
- Aidan hops back into his mom's van. Mom tells us that the girl is new, and clearly very experienced. She asks him about her, and he realizes he doesn't know, which is very odd. He clocks via profiling that she's operating out of raw talent, not skill, that she's never skateboarded before, yet is a fast learner.

## Entry Exam

- He gets home, showers, eats dinner, and falls asleep. Next morning, he opens his laptop to find his browser open to a webpage. **THE ACADEMY GREETS YOU, AIDAN TRAVIS. YOU HAVE CAUGHT OUR INTEREST. YOU HAVE 48 HOURS TO DO THE IMPOSSIBLE IF YOU CHOOSE TO ACCEPT THE CHALLENGE.** After going back and forth, he resolves that even if this is a trojan, he has backups of all his files, so he could just restore it. He clicks yes. **WE WARN YOU, ONCE YOU CHOOSE TO BEGIN, THERE WILL BE NO TURNING BACK.**
- He is greeted by the image of a Middle Eastern man in casual clothing in the middle of the desert, busy market behind him in the background, yet he stands alone. The photo has been taken by a Polaroid camera. He looks to see a button labeled **Archives**, and one labeled **Notes**. Clicking on Archives, he is face to face with thousands of classified documents from CIA internal archives. No turning back now, he's guilty of treason.
- 48 hours of high stress psychological thriller as a prodigy profiler gets deep inside the head of this man, teaching us how his powers work. He builds internal models of people's behavior in his mind, predicting what they would do, and communicating with this model in his head to gather information. Learn about a person without talking to them. He is pushed harder than he ever has been in his life, and is so exhausted after passing that he doesn't even dream as he sleeps for 18 hours.
- Izzy shows up in his bedroom in a college uniform. He has questions, but his mind won't work, so he settles for greeting her. "You have many questions. I believe this should answer all of them. Welcome to The Academy, Aidan Travis. What you have accomplished is no small feat."

# Act II — So I Have Friends Now

## Welcome To The Academy

- Aidan immediately profiles that Izzy has black belts, multiple of them, and when he indirectly asks how many, she comments that she hates profilers. The first time we get the idea that profiler === person who can figure out things you don't want them to know. He connects this with her remark about being worried she was "turning into Emma" at her appreciation for thrills. Is Emma a profiler? Is that a bad thing?
- When Izzy mentions that his parents are handling the paperwork, she also mentions that she's also… "yes, stroking their egos". Manipulation is not the exception at The Academy. It's the standard, like a fish noticing water.
- Aidan meets The Academy. He understands that the Academy building is less of an Academy and more a fortress meant to endure a siege. The story of a man hiding in the most unfindable place on Earth to run away from demons is obvious to Aidan, but of course he has no confidence in this assessment because he's a 16 year old with no formal training who hasn't even taken a formal psychology course. Something something telling everyone a reactor blew up based on the description of a rock.
- Izzy demonstrates absurd athleticism, but Aidan can sense intense dread. She's holding it together, barely. A girl asks for the password. "The password is: Emma is a—" and then uses a slur that Aidan dare not repeat. Twenty seconds after seeing Aidan through the camera, Emma has already profiled his deepest fears and forces a kiss out of him that he definitely doesn't _not_ enjoy. The Mommy thing, something he barely recognizes as a thing in himself and has never told anyone, is figured out within seconds as if this is the most casual thing in the world. Aidan now understands why Izzy hates profilers.
- After Christopher identifies himself as himself and the one listening to him during his entrance exam by clarifying they are not a Neo-Nazy terrorist sect and he is very aware the Holocaust happened, we get a single line that explains him: "You have a Hyprland configuration that's seventeen files but still use Google Chrome. I am beyond words."
- Shameless Artemis Fowl reference. Aidan talks with Emma as she shows him around the Academy, and he finally finds somebody that… actually talks to him at his wavelength, noticing the things he notices, and does something even crazier: She lays out the truths about himself that he doesn't dare face the moment he dares tries to read her past. Not as a way of keeping him away, but making sure he understands what he's asking for. He refers to it as a Truly Intelligent Conversation, but this time it isn't arrogance like Fowl's. These are two neurodivergent people, able to play the YA romance trope straight because they are talking in the same language as regular teens on a macro scale yet saying completely different things.

## I've Never Felt So Alive

- After becoming a full member of The Academy, he returns to the same bedroom Emma showed him, but now it's different. He has a briefing now. A task. He's being challenged. He's actually getting excited, especially now that he's meeting the others and living what he sees as the fantasy life. We're meeting characters in their daily lives.:
  - Christopher is a tech/chess/poker/statistics prodigy whose mind works so fast that he's constantly pushing himself and his body to catch up, parallel to Izzy's relationship with her own body.
  - Jamie is a perfectionist art prodigy that can detect a forgery a mile away on intuition even if it takes her days to prove it. Her room, an art studio with a dressing room whose dresses are exactly 3/4 inch apart, has sketchbooks organized by tone and palette. This place _is_ her space, because it _is_ her. She is constantly growing familiarity with artists and obsessed with being exactly right, painting and sketching and drawing constantly to hone her own craft. Aidan asks her what she's building toward. "Ditto". Not "Shut up", but "you don't know what you're asking".
  - Emma is… a sociopath. She wears the term confidently, acting impulsively and doing weird stuff because she doesn't care what other people think. She knows it's outdated, clinicians haven't used the term for decades, but "if people on the Autism Spectrum can tell the world they are Aspies, I can call myself a Sociopath". She grew up to an abusive and neglectful father with a hair trigger, learning not to think ahead because any plan could get thrown out the window by a bad mood in sixty minutes. She retracted away from empathy because worrying about hurting people was stopping her from surviving. Learned to read and manipulate people in seconds to minutes so she could feed herself… and also steal what she wanted. At 12 years old, she was forced to kill her own father to stay alive. She wasn't traumatized by the _murder_, though, she was disgusted by the blood and bone, promising to never do it again. Serving as proof that "in the long game, the actions of the egoist and the altruist converge to the same sum".
  - Robin is a reckless independent confident she can build her way out of anything. A prodigy engineer, mechanic and chemist just wanting to help people even if her own arrogance sometimes gets in her way. She likes proving people wrong. Her room is a workshop so filled with safety hazards it would make an OSHA employee drop dead of shock, which she seems to delight in. She is determined to a fault, working for months on her inventions, so focused on her own work that she really doesn't think through the consequences when she drives a Camaro straight off the lot to take it apart and figure out how it works or shoplifts a gear out of a store because she needs it more. Her ingenuity and compassion has attracted multiple partners over the years, but she's so intensely focused on her own work that it frequently becomes transactional and partners leave. She's a good person who makes a lot of mistakes… what do you mean she scores a 27 on the PCL-R?
  - Alex is a prodigy linguist, polyglot, codebreaker and writer. He is highly social and likes RP and D&D, yet has attachment issues and finds it difficult to let go. He seeks precision in his words and language, often relying on foreign languages like German, Dutch, and Russian to find the right word for the job, parallel to Jamie's search for precision in art and getting things exactly right. Alex, too, often finds it difficult to speak slowly but he's far less visual and far more concrete, being far more literal to contrast Jamie's abstraction.
- His reads of the others plant the idea that The Director using a middleman to communicate is highly unusual, and reads of Izzy confirm that her father is not merely out but in hiding. Still, he lacks confidence in his own abilities outside of the puzzle he's told to solve, trusting book-related knowledge over in-the-moment knowledge, and ignores warning signs.
- Aidan slowly begins to crack away at the puzzle he's been given, learning about this Russian mobster, his life, and how he could've possibly gone dark. He tries to carry his models about Middle Eastern terrorists and cult leaders into this new puzzle, but they don't work, so he grows frustrated but also excited. Being wrong about this doesn't surprise him, but it does worry him, because he dreads the possibility of finally losing his place right when it matters most. He mirrors Alex's attachment issues.:
  - Emma is the one person who mirrors him in his ability to read people, solving his emotional needs, building a relationship, serving as a peer, but she teaches him that he needs to be confident in his assessments of people. He needs to learn to trust himself. "You will be wrong sometimes. Nobody is perfect. But that doesn't mean your input doesn't matter. You are here to learn, and you are here because you are better at what you do than anybody else in the world with very few exceptions."
  - Alex is the one person who mirrors his desperate need for connection, fear of being wrong manifesting as fear of getting rejected because he offends them, but he sees Alex's issues. He senses the insecurity. Yet also sees the other side. Alex is an invaluable member of the team, with parents that don't understand him but want him to succeed, and a family here that would move the world if that's what he needs. Alex and Aidan hug. Being a profiler means a rare ability to see the needs of others, and the way The Academy handles things means that every social rule is purely optional. You take what you need, discard what you don't. A total, radical acceptance of people where they are, who they are, what they need, and an inherent willingness to teach. Yes, they are teens, but at the end of the day, in terms of their abilities, they have better communication skills than most adults. Masterful manipulation is the rule, expert negotiation is the expectation, and so every interaction is highlighted with people giving things that mean very little to them but a lot to other people. Listening, actively, before they talk. Being willing to teach. The great subversion: An Academy of monsters who have all learned to be kind without any expectation of reward.
  - Jamie is the one person who mirrors his need to get everything exactly right, his cognitive delays in speaking, his instinctive abstraction, as well as his innate need to know where he's going. She talks to him about thinking about the future, teaching him fashion, translating her internal knowledge about color theory and styles into something he can use. This helps him, because it allows him to understand things he's never understood before. He varies. Sometimes, he's convinced he has no idea what he's doing and he's going to fail, and other times, he's wholly convinced he has everything right or that certain things are just impossible to figure out so they aren't worth learning. "You aren't going to offend me. I practically grew up with Emma. So… Sherlock… profile me. Right now. Even if you're wrong. Just read, don't critique."
  - Robin is the answer. She's his aspiration. Helpful, kind, resourceful, kinetic, and even though she's strong-willed and sometimes arrogant she has a good heart. She's determined and unrelenting, similar in her low tolerance of failure but instead embracing it. She's constantly building something. Constantly cheering people up. She knows not just how to read a room, but how to find the right words to resolve conflict. She affectionately calls him Cassie, she compliments his work, always is the one to notice how much effort he's putting in, always supportive and affirming and helpful. She loves teaching him what she knows. But she also never seems to have anything dark within her, even though she's a member of The Academy.

## Something Isn't Adding Up…

- As the team gets together, is becomes clear that this is their hardest challenge to date, but also that there are just enough details between the people they are going after in common to set of alarm bells that something is wrong. Really wrong. Izzy is genuinely dreading what's coming. Christopher and Aidan are exchanging notes about ciphers. Robin is cheery and relentlessly positive, but he can sense something is off about her. Jamie is starting to get concerned. Something is coming.
- Emma and Aidan are growing into each other. Aidan is learning more about how to read emotions and how social cues work, being taught confidence and taught about her. Emma is learning empathy and managing feelings she doesn't quite understand. Yet this isn't a typical romance. A romance between a sociopath profiler and an Autistic profiler was never going to be normal. They are blunt with each other. She thinks she has romantic feelings for him, he also thinks he has romantic feelings for her. They start dating to figure out what this is, as if this is the most casual thing in the world, because to them it is. He's terrified of not doing this right. She isn't scared. They are deeply vulnerable with each other. Jamie is learning coping strategies for her OCD, Alex is learning how to process and heal his attachment issues, Christopher is learning meditation the ADHD way to find out how to slow down, Robin is learning how to be serious and push against the group when she need to.

## Oh God No

- Aidan, Emma, Robin, Christopher, Jamie, Izzy and Alex learn at the same time that they've all been hunting the same man. Robert Rosenbloom. Izzy realizes they've been tricked. Then the alarms go off, and Izzy demands they get to the safe room, leaving the briefcases behind. She heads into the security room to see what the source is, and when she returns… she incapacitates all of them in three seconds flat.

## We're Not Going To Make It

- They find Rosenbloom's location, but have no way of getting there. They panic. Robin attempts to comfort everyone, but it doesn't work. Then Izzy opens a drawer, calmly picks up a phone, and begins speaking in perfect Russian, listed out coordinates and giving orders over the line like it's nothing. Afterwards, she looks shaken and regretful, like what she did may get them all killed.
- With nothing left to do but wait, they prep the medical bay with Emma's help to receive Rosenbloom, and Izzy… begins to explain things.
- Rosenbloom returns by heli and is placed in the medical bay. Emma treats the wounds and stabilizes him as paramedics arrive, explaining the full extent of his injuries and rattling of his blood type and how to treat him. Then Emma hugs Aidan, and explains that _that_ was the first time in years she had felt truly afraid.

## We Made It, But This Isn't The End

- After an alert is raised in Robin's workshop, Aidan, Alex, Christopher and Jamie run across The Academy to see Robin crying as she points to the burned body of Janus. A chemical fire. Robin mistook the chemical burning for fabric burning, and dumped water, accelerating the fire and killing him within seconds. Alex, Christopher and Jamie comfort her as Aidan calls the police. Once the others leave, Aidan realizes it wasn't an accident. A prodigy chemist does not simply mis-label a chemical fire in the heat of the moment. Robin deliberately murdered Janus, and she's on the verge of becoming a serial killer.
- After Rosenbloom sternly explains that Izzy should not have called on his old friends for help, Izzy insists she had no choice and if she didn't he'd be dead within hours. He reveals that every single one of them had an entrance exam that required them to commit treason at one point. As the old man said, he says, Trust, but verify. Janus was never power-hungry. He was always a coward. They just put themselves on the map with the worst demons on the planet, but if they go to a government for help, they become threats to national security.
