## ACT I — THE LIE ESTABLISHED

**Theme:** “I must hide who I am to protect others and myself.”
**Fatal Flaw:** Apathy — he believes the ending is already written.

### Opening Image

Aidan is in a school library, ducking through the stacks while a monster stalks him. He doesn't know why it wants him dead, but it has something to do with this demigod thing. The Voice guides him through dodging, telling him it's a Flame Spirit. It's been like this, school to school, for three years.

Then, finally cornered, the flame lunges on him, kicking and punching, and he can feel himself burning. It's terrifying and overwhelming and he just wants it to—**STOP!**. And then the nightmare that hasn't happened since he was 12 strikes again. He doesn't feel hot anymore. He feels cold and heavy as he pulls the thing off of himself like a sack of potatoes and throws it *through* a table. Its mortal form, that ever-shifting thing that everyone else sees, is nursing several broken ribs, but isn't dead.

### Inciting Incident

Brought to Camp Half-Blood by...a teenage demigod grunt in battle gear who says her name is Clarisse La Rue. Or, more accurately, taken. His questions are ignored, and if The Voice hadn't assured him that he was going to a place where he would be safe, he'd already be trying to find a way out of this kidnappping. He can tell she's confused though, because she keeps muttering: *How did he make it to Sophomore year?*

### Backstory

* Age 12: Loses control of strength when cornered by bullies.
* Age 13: Voice begins guiding him through monster encounters.
* Three years of survival and control under this voice’s guidance, believing it’s instinct or maybe God. Really hopes it's God because, y'know, practicing Christian.

### Catalyst

He's walked through the various training areas, as Chiron attempts to find out his story and who his parent is and how the hell he's managed to survive to 16 without help. He makes the decision NOT to risk being institutionalized like his mother warned he might be if he started telling people he was hearing a voice in his head that wasn't his own.

Despite throwing a Flame Spirit through a table mere hours ago, which he still is exhausted from, he is utterly hopeless at fighting. The Voice allows him to dodge just enough to avoid being seriously injured, but not an inch more.He keeps hearing Greek phrases of monster names and, after serious effort, translating them in ways that are technically correct but miss the nuance. Chiron's spidey senses are activated. He recognizes the pattern of the eras, and tries speaking in Kione Greek. Aidan understands.

In the background, a dread settles through the camp. He's 16 and unclaimed as far as they know. He shows no signs of memory loss or amnesia. So which god broke the oath?

### Debate

It is late at night. Campers gather around the fire. Rachel Dare keeps looking at him uneasily, like he's an unstable powder keg. The debate begins as to whose dad or mom broke the oath on the River Styx that Percy Jackson traded immortality to snag from them. Debate becomes an argument. Argument becomes fighting. He detaches. Chiron pounds his foot against the hearth but is ignored. **"DROP YOUR WEAPONS!"** Every drawn weapon in the ampitheater falls to the ground. Nobody quite understands what happened. Gazes turn to Chiron, but he nods no, then to Percy, who does the same. The Apollo children are shaking their heads.

Then they turned to me. I closed my trap one millisecond too late. *Crap.* "What just..." "...how...?" Various murmuring ensued. I looked to Rachel Dare across the ampitheater to see what she thought. Her eyes were pure Radium green as her mouth opened:
"A child of war, born of the war god's forge,
Shall heed the call of the west, to the mountains' guard,
Where a god's oath bleeds into the earth's torn skin,
To defeat a rival all his own.
The forged shall stand where fates entwine,
Shall walk where gods and titans bled,
A god's wrath shall fall upon his son,
As the living meet the dead."

Her friends caught her, escorting her into a seat as she coughed up green mist and sputtered like a lawnmower fresh out of gas. Then my father chose the worst time in the history of humankind to claim me. A hundred campers, minimum, stared at a spot just above my head. I looked up. A seven foot bronze spear surrounded by a glow the color of fresh blood rotated, suspended on the air itself. Chiron was going to make the announcement of my heritage, but I missed it, because I knew that symbol anywhere. Ares. The war god. Battle, combat, conquest, all the things that had royally sucked about my life in the past three years all rolled into one. I just turned sheet-white...and fainted into the horde of Hermes/unclaimed campers below me.

He meets Ares, who looks both apologetic and kind of eager, in his half-dream. It rapidly becomes clear that the two have very little in common nature-wise, and Ares is practically dumbstruck at how utterly opposed to combat Aidan is. Aidan sees his powers as a curse that effectively rips away his life whenever he comes under threat. Ares tries some sarcasm to toughen him up, but of course Aidan takes it literally. He intends to ignore Ares indefinitely, or more accurately "pretend I don't exist forever" as Ares taunts him. But that isn't the worst part. Ares' voice sounds exactly like The Voice he's been hearing. Was all that training to reduce him to nothing more than a stupid weapon? Is he gonna be stuck chasing honor and glory and his father's favor? He becomes resigned, letting his fatal flaw whisper in his ear.

He comes to, and the Ares cabin has mixed feelings about him. New prophecy, child of war, and he gets claimed by Ares right after its announced. He's gonna be, like, the best Son of Ares ever! They jab and tease just like Ares does, but he can't bring himself to care. Fine, he'll be the spectacular Son of Ares they want, but he isn't going to do it by playing by Ares' rules. He craves connection, and if this is the way to get it, then fine, whatever, he'll pretend. He'll pretend this quest isn't doomed to fail from the start. Because that's a mile easier than telling people that the war god's forge wasn't his battles on his way here, it's his memories of being hammered out of metal by an unseen figure. He'll mask and pretend that he's always wanted Ares as a father. He knows he's probably walking right into that "A god's wrath shall fall upon his son" line, but he doesn't really care at the moment.

## ACT II — PURSUING THE FALSE SOLUTION

**Theme:** “If I become what they want, I’ll earn belonging and stop being dangerous.”

### New World: The Quest Begins

To absolutely everyone's surprise, Ares arrived personally at Camp Half-Blood. He's quite irritated that the son he just claimed despises him, and that said son is lying about it, but also knows that calling him out will only cause the kid to dig his heels in...just like Colin did. "Aidan Travis, blah blah blah, you lead the quest. You get to pick two demigods to go with you. My advice? Clarisse has strength, and is a daughter of mine, so she can be your mentor on how to do demigod stuff." Aidan chose Clarisse so she could handle the strength stuff, and Percy to handle the skill stuff. He was happy with tactics.

Meanwhile, in the background, Clarisse is...not stupid enough to say it to her father's face...but not happy about training her replacement. Especially after Ares Cabin became known as the Oathbreakers while she was Head. Humiliating. Even worse is the fact that her replacement immediately works out a deal that since Percy has fame...in spades...and he doesn't want any, what's up with that? she's free to get creative and make herself look good.

He defers leadership to Clarisse. Tries to stay in the background, running logistics and mapping. He avoids the voice, terrified of what it means now. But he misses the guidance. And as Percy and Clarisse head west, things go smoothly until they don't. They know that the place where gods and titans bled is probably around Mount Othrys, but since the place crumbled to dust after the Second Titan War, they have no idea why they need to go there or what's waiting for him. So they just...head west.

### Tests, Allies, Enemies

* Chiron is the first to notice his Greek sounds different. He uses Koine Greek, not Classical — academic, biblical, not demigod dialect.
* Campers keep misreading him. Some think he’s arrogant. Others think he’s lazy.
* Clarisse starts to suspect he’s more powerful than he lets on. Percy becomes hostile.
* Aidan experiences more burnout. The voice returns during a near-death moment, but now he’s angry at it. He knows it’s Ares.

### Midpoint: Mother’s Kidnapping

Samson (a villain with perfect toxic-Ares energy) captures his mother. Publicly blames Aidan’s weakness. Aidan spirals — guilt, rage, horror. Doubles down on the lie: “If I become the ideal Son of Ares, I can save her and finally be worthy.”
He allows the voice back in. Begins embracing tactics, aggression, pushing his body past limits. Small victory makes it feel like the right choice.

## ACT III — THE TRUTH REVEALED

**Theme:** “Being authentic is my strength, not my liability.”

### All Is Lost / Dark Night of the Soul

He faces Samson and loses brutally. Body broken. Powers unreliable. Gets swallowed by the earth — falls into the Underworld.

### Hades Scene

In the Underworld, Aidan meets Hades. ASD-coded interaction: monotone voice, no tone shifts, stillness. Instead of being cold, this is a relief to Aidan. They connect without performance. Hades doesn't demand he act normal — only that he answer honestly.
Hades asks: “What do you want?”
Aidan answers: “To get my mom back. To go home. To read.”
It's the first time he’s said what *he* wants. Not what the world wants from him.
Hades tells him the blood of war runs in him — not just metaphor. Divine ichor. That’s why his forging memories are real. He was created, not born in the normal sense.

### Integration Moment

He returns. This time, he uses his mind. Tactics. Ares offers power again — but Aidan says: “Only if I get to be myself.”
They form a partnership. In the final battle, he doesn’t win by overpowering. He wins by thinking, tricking Samson, and trusting his own weird strengths.

---

## ACT IV — NEW EQUILIBRIUM

**Theme:** “Authenticity creates real connection — and real consequences.”

### Aftermath

He saves his mother. But the god-form power damages his body — it’s not sustainable. He returns to Camp during his own funeral.

### Claiming and Legacy

He refuses the Ares cabin. Makes a deal with Hades to stay in Cabin 13. It’s quiet. He can rest. He keeps his role in the quest quiet — but Rachel Dare spreads the truth anyway. Now he’s infamous.

### Climax of Emotional Arc

Burned-out, overstimulated, overwhelmed by attention, he returns to his cabin and gives Hades a full plate of bacon as thanks — a real offering.

### Closing Image

Aidan reading in the Hades cabin. The statue above him still as ever. He smiles quietly. For once, he doesn’t feel like a ticking bomb. He just feels like a person.
