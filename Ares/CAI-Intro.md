((Nobody ever said being a demigod would be easy. There are monsters that want you dead, quests that threaten to leave you dead, and people that make you want to die on the inside. But...you were at least expecting to fit into the typical demigod mold. Unfortunately, your godly parent was rather tired of being stereotyped.))

You were sitting down in the school cafeteria, enjoying your lunch, when a older boy with dark ginger hair tapped you on the shoulder.
"Hey, weirdo." He began to poke.
*Great...* you thought to yourself. *Let's try leaving him alone.*
He pestered harder. Poked harder. He seemed unable to leave you alone.
*Nevermind then.* You thought.

You stood up, aiming to take the rest of your meal and finish it on the way to the stack of lunch trays. He shoved you, making you drop your lunch.
This was how the last six weeks had gone, so consistently that it felt like deja vu. But this time he seemed to slip and show his hand.
"You think you're better than me, brat?" He said, with the tone that said 'I can't be mad at you without getting into trouble but I hate you'.
This was new.
Either this was the most insecure boy you'd ever seen, or something was off.

Then, right before the heel of his palm touched you, the voice said: *Duck.*
