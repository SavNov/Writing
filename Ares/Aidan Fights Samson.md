The huge mountains seemed to stretch endlessly on either side. The fortress was…not fully guarded. That should've been the first indication that something was already wrong about this.
I stepped forward, brandishing the sword I'd been given. Small, light, something I could reasonably use without much training.
Three guards stood at the entrance.
*They are shaken. Something has happened. Something that has rattled their confidence.* Ares said.
One of them had a strap undone in their armor as I approached.
The one on the left, strap fully attached, grinned at me weakly.
"What are you here for?" He asked, mockery clear in his tone.
"I am here for a meeting with Samson." I said.
All three cracked up laughing.
"What? You? You must be joking!"
"Tell him I'm the Child of War he's been expecting." I said, my voice naturally calm, flat and even, unwavering.
They looked at each other.
"Okay, sure, if you are really this Child of War from the prophecy, prove it then! Get past us! Don't worry, we'll go—" The man on the left said, but before he could finish his sentence, I swung my sword.
*Left. Before he gets his guard up. Hit the chestplate, knock him off his balance.* I thought.
He leaned to the left a few inches, then stabilized himself.
"Well played, kid. Now, let's actually—" He tried again.
What followed was a chain of commands from Ares, and I followed from memory. Thirty monsters defeated had taught me how to perform each move individually, but I'd never chained them together before.
*Left. Uppercut. Strike down, 45 degrees. Dodge left. Sweep with feet clockwise. 90 degree turn right, duck to dodge sword swing, strike opponent wrist, disarm. Dodge right, allow opponent blades to jam together. Strike upward to break lock and destabilize blades, kick upward to break weakened hold. 30 degree turn left, parry incoming blow to abdomen with side swipe, kick downwards, lunge up, strike helmet with force.*
I looked at the three men. Two now laid on the ground, disarmed, and the third was nursing a nasty concussion.
*Objective achieved.* Ares said, sounding proud.
*We are not out of the woods yet.* I thought back.
