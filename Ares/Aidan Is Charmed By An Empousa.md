AIDAN
You are not supposed to get physical with a monster. Especially not with one trying to kill you.
— said no touch-starved demigod, who didn't even know if he could die, ever.
He had no idea what that younger great-grandchild of Zeus, because that was how the boy referred to himself, had done to Percy and Clarisse's brains, because he wasn't a neuroscientist and clearly that boy wasn't either, but he was *pretty sure* sending the son of the sea god into a full-blown catatonic seizure by simply screwing with the electricity between his neurons had screwed with his brain a bit. Even after they'd *left* behind the child of Dionysus who weirdly insisted on being referred to as a son of Bacchus and speaking in Latin at certain points—Percy attributed this to madness—and his mind games, none of them had been acting right.
Electricity was suddenly very important. Like how Percy remarked that the human body was 70% water, electricity kept a lot of the things in the body moving. Like the heart, as Clarisse had found out. And the brain, as Percy had discovered.
It was horrifying the amount of damage electrokinesis could do simply by taking the electricity in one part of the body and moving it a few inches over.

Children of Ares were not supposed to have to give CPR to their quest mates because some demigod had decided to try evil out today and send a child of Ares into cardiac arrest.
She was alive now, but still nowhere near lucid.
"Percy…" She said, like what she said was vital information important for saving the world, pointing to a blank ceiling tile high above them. "Don't step on the spider! He's driving his little truck with a widdle-bitty city in the back…"
Percy charged toward the Empousa with her arms around him.
"Have to kill. Have to kill it. Too dangerous. For—" He said, only to pause ten seconds in at the feet of the Empousa, collapsing to the ground, tears running down his cheeks. "—Beckendorf!"
His knees buckled as he sank to the ground in a heap, sobbing.
"Told him it was the only way! Was it? My fault! Should've saved him!"
Riptide hovered inches above where his heart would be, poised to strike with lethal intent.
"Need to pay…"
"Percy, now you're just being—" Clarisse grunted, pointing at a tile on the ceiling I now realized she saw Percy standing on like gravity had decided to take a vacation.
"…pay. Play? Yeah, let's play a game! C'mon guys, let's go!" Percy said, descending into manic laughter, crying with joy as he sheathed Riptide and gestured for them to follow.
I wanted to laugh at them, but honestly whatever had happened to me made me feel really, *really good*, just so relaxed and happy and calm.
"Ha…you're funny Percy. I'm sure he'll be…*yawn*…fine…yeah…fine man…heh…Feynmann…good one…" I said.
"Shhh…" The Empousa whispered, placing a tender finger to my lips.
*Don't you do it.* Ares warned.
*I am going to do it.* I thought back.
*Don't you dare.* Ares warned.
"It's okay. I have to get Samson here so he can fight you himself, and your friends still have yet to kill each other, but I don't have to do any of that *now*…" She said, her voice impossibly soft as she wrapped her arms around me. The girl was probably 17 thousand years old, but barely looked 17. Her arms were around my neck as she eyed said neck, baring her fangs.

*Why not?* I thought at Ares.
*Her kind were created to seduce mortal men to their deaths. Just as she's trying to do with you right now.* Ares argued.
*Since we still don't know if I even **can** die, I'm willing to take that chance.* I thought.
*She is seducing you.* Ares grunted, very much done with this level of stupidity.
*And I want to be seduced. Where's the problem?* I countered.
*Gods of Olympus, boy, you're almost as bad as Clarisse…* Ares sighed.
I leaned my head against her shoulder. Her fire-y hair glowed faintly amber as she leaned down.
"Don't worry, little demigod. I've got you now. No need to be scared. I want this too." She purred.
Her skin was incredibly soft through that simple t-shirt and khaki shorts. I nuzzled into the crook of her neck like a little boy.
"You poor thing…" She crooned.
I felt her fangs *prick* me slightly as she sank them gently into my neck like a tender vampire, she began drinking. I didn't mind that. I felt so fuzzy-wuzzy and warm right now that it felt like nothing mattered.
In, and out, in, and out.

Percy and Clarisse stared at me in pure, unbridled shock. Though how much of it was actual shock and how much of it was crazy was unclear.
"Is he seriously…?" Percy asked.
"There's no way…" Clarisse sputtered in disbelief.
Their eyes met, Percy and Clarisse together.
"Has this happened on…?" I began.
"Any quest I've been on? No. You?"
Percy nodded no.

Her warm fingers brushed into the muscles of my back as I was vaguely aware that there was a certain amount of blood the heart required to keep everything going smoothly. I knew I was nowhere near that threshold, but still.
"Can you just…" I asked, that voice all flat and soft, but not as stony as before.
Her fingers tied and tangled my hair into knots.
"You are an adorable little demigod…shame my master has to kill you." She said in a gentle, forlorn purr.
"For…this? I'm willing to…live with that…" I yawned. She swayed me back and forth. It was hypnotically as I yawned again almost immediately.
Ares was too stunned to even form words.
"Can we just…" I muttered in a daze.
She whispered in my ear.
"Stay like this for a while? Of course. As long as you don't go anywhere, we can stay like this as long as you like…"
I nodded, content with the arrangement.

And so I cuddled with an Empousa as she drank my blood.
…
The Empousa screams in agony as the divine blood burns through her.
"You---you worthless son of a---I will get you for this! Does it make you proud to have defeated me? To know that you killed me with the very thing I need to live? Huh? Are you proud that your little mommy is sitting in Samson's harem because you had to play hero for a few days? Are you?"
He steps forward, changing in an instant.
"Say what?" He asks. The cat is out of the proverbial bag now. They know he's not entirely human anymore.
Might as well give up on trying to ignore it, then.
His voice turns cold and dead and flat. Like how he naturally talks, but with a far darker inflection now.
The Empousa tried to vanish into the fire, but he grabbed her by the throat as the familiar golden lines burned into his skin for the first time since the day he'd gotten to Camp Half-Blood. (Golden lines are the indicator of starting to lean on the Ares inside of him, pulling on that power)
"Say that again. Slowly." He growled.
The Empousa struggled to breath under his grip.
"I said your mommy is sitting in Samson's harem because you...had to---gack..." She grunted, only to feel his grip tighten again as the pressure on her throat evolved into a slight choke. "...play the hero--"
The Empousa was choking now. He tightened the grip until the familiar cracking sound and watched her disintegrate into dust.
"Playtime's over." He grunted.
Though he didn't know it, his eyes burned red with anger.
"I'm going to kill that boy myself." He said, standing taller.
He looked at Percy and Clarisse. Percy looked uneasy. Clarisse looked deeply disturbed.
He no longer cared.
