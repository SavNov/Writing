AIDAN
As the world faded to black, I wasn't at the campfire anymore. I wasn't even in the infirmary.

I was in a war room. To my left and right, huge columns guarded rack after rack of weapons. Spears, clubs, swords, Chinese rockets and Roman chariots.

*What is this place?* I thought.

The voice gave no answer.

I walked forward, and kept walking forward.
