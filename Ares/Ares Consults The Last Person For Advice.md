ARES
The god of war is not supposed to afraid.

Ares stood at his desk overlooking his war room on Mount Olympus. The place was grand, built by Elder Cyclopes he did not care to know the names of before, with huge white columns ringed with celestial bronze, the palatial floor glittering under the light of the flames of the huge forge at the end of the room. It had been a stupid idea.

*Haphaestus isn't special.* He'd whined. *The god of war needs a place to forge weapons too.*
