Because my mind is not adjusted to the difficulty of starting from a blank page. I don't bake, I carve. And right now my best, most lengthy writing has been when I was stuck in the car or the bus with nothing better to do, where I just sat with the task until it was done. This is how I got to the point of reading so quickly and reading so much. There was a segment of my day, thirty minutes in the morning and in the evening, where I had nothing better to do so I just...read. And read. And read. I just allowed my mind to run.
