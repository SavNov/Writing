# Jason

## Background

- Son of Jupiter (Roman god) and Beryl Grace
- Brother (does not know) of Thalia Grace ([Thalia Grace][1])
- Abandoned by mother at the ([Wolf House][2]) at age of 2.
- Joined the Legion at age 5.
- Age 11 during The Eagle Thief.
- Age 12 during Wrath of Neptune.
- Age 13 during Ghoul's Vow.
- Age 13-14 during Tale of Two.
- Age 14-15 during Augur's Deal.

## Powers

- Wind manipulation.
- Superhuman strength.
- Flight (from wind manipulation).
- Electro-kinesis.
- Can summon lightning at most once per day.
- Expert swordsman.
- Formidable leader.

## Personality

- Jason is honorable, duty-bound and mission-focused, looking calm and stoic on the surface but inside is wracked with internal conflict.
- He is split between his desire to be a normal Roman demigod and his duties as both a Son of Jupiter and later a Centurion, and even later a Praetor.
- He is also split between his Roman training and restraint… and his inner instincts and gut feelings and Greek heritage that he can't explain.
- Jason cannot help but side with underdogs and bet on dark horses, naturally drawn towards those whom nobody looks at and nobody thinks are that strong or smart.
- Jason's greatest fear is that one day he will lose control of his own strength and kill those around him in the collateral damage. As such, he is always holding back his strength and powers to avoid hurting those he cares about.

## Friends

- Jason, at the start, is friends with Connor Davies, but it's never been on equal footing. Jason's reputation as a Son of Jupiter makes him a near-alien to the rest of the Fifth Cohort, a living legend rather than a person.
- Jason's Praetor is Gwendolyn, 15 years old at the start of Eagle Thief. Her mantra is: If you wish to know the measure of a man, give him power. If you wish to make a man great, let him learn to fail forward. She, though he remains unaware of this, is always forcing Jason to act even when it guarantees failure because she wishes to make him conquer his fear of failure by failing and learning that failure is not his enemy but instead his teacher.

## Eagle Thief

- After Lupa explains to Jason during early morning training with Gwen that "demigods have always been of two camps. Two sides. And what rises in the East always settles in the West." and that something important has gone missing in the East, meaning that something equally important may go missing at Camp Jupiter, Jason dreads that he knows exactly what is happening, and is proven right.
- The First Cohort's Eagle goes missing. Jason is without lightning powers or wind manipulation, believing that his father has turned his back on him, though is proven wrong later on.
- Gwen marches Jason at sword point to the Temple of Jupiter, where Octavian, Augur-in-all-but-name and about to be named Augur in the Senate, is cutting up stuffed animals and reading old prophecies out of a codex. Jason, filled with self-doubt and ignoring his instincts, reports his suspicions to Octavian that the Eagle has been stolen, not just gone missing. Octavian is surprised by this, but Jason's gut tells him that Octavian knows more than he appears to.:
  - Octavian provides Jason the following prophecy.

  ```text
  You shall go west, to face the god who has turned,
  You shall find what was stolen, and see it safely returned.
  You shall be betrayed by one who calls you a friend,
  And you shall lose what matters most in the end.
  ```

- Octavian rushes off to inform the Senate of Jason's suspicions, as well as a few of his own.
- Praetor Gwen calls a quest for Jason, knowing the Senate won't do anything until days later, but still needs a Centurion to lead the quest in accordance with Roman law, so she reluctantly chooses Dakota of the Fifth Cohort.
- Jason sets off into the San Francisco Bay Area, following his training and planning skills, determined to go on a quest, take back the Fifth Cohort's Eagle, get back in his father's good graces again, and get his power over the winds and lightning back.
- He goes to the house of Frederick Chase, the one mortal in the Bay Area obsessed enough with the winds and history to actually own a Sopwith Camel biplane, and who has been studying wind anomalies for days now apparently. Discovers that Annabeth, the daughter of Frederick that Jason knows is a demigod of some kind but not more than that (and has never met), is also on a quest for something very powerful and very dangerous… confirming Jason's dreaded instincts again, along with this Percy fellow that Jason has never heard of.
- While there, Jason's attempt to use an old artifact for contacting Jupiter, and succeeds, only to find that he both hasn't lost his father's favor and isn't getting his powers back anytime soon. Jupiter's Master Bolt has gone missing, meaning no lightning in the sky unless you can possibly find another lightning-generating relic in the Bay Area, and with basically a Roman nuke dropping off the map all orders besides ones relating to finding it are to be ignored. Meaning the winds are going to ignore Jason's orders until the Master Bolt is found.
- Jason sees on the news that a youth at the New York Metropolitan Museum has just destroyed several pieces of art, and of course the media is spinning it so he looks like a troubled youth. Jason sees the word boy as 'νεαρό αγόρι', the Greek term, and when Praetor Gwen says she senses Erinyes, Jason corrects her by calling them Furies, the Greek term. She is confused, but moves past it.
