Jason had made failing forward his motto, just like Praetor Gwen before him and Praetor Samuel before her. He trusted Praetor Reyna's judgement. She was a wild one, but she'd learned to restrain herself.

He knew she had feelings for him. He knew they were unrequited. He just didn't see her like that, and didn't want to leave her feeling used.

Greek and Roman were finally blurring together. Things were stirring. His secret visit to the Delphi cave had taught him that. A Greek place. He was drawn to it now, like a moth to a flame, except Jason was fireproof.

It had been a failing to come here, to the middle of the desert. An old truck. Telling no one. Trusting his instincts like Reyna had taught him.

A failing he intended to learn from. As the old Praetor had once said, "If you wish to make a man great, let him learn to fail forward.".

A Greyhound. A crash. A boy. A girl. A splitting headache that made hammered Reyna in the White Dragon look tame.

"You okay, man? You look… roughed up." The boy said. Jason sensed it immediately. Fire. Forge. Furnace. Tinkerer. Son of Vulcan… no.

Not Vulcan. Hephaestus.

"Leo, of course he's not okay! Look at him!" The girl said.

Shifting eyes, purple and green and blue and every color in-between. Beauty. Change.

Venus. No, not Venus either. Aphrodite.

He dared lift his head, groaning as he did so.

"Who—?" He tried to ask, but his voice didn't work.

"This is Leo." The girl said. "I'm Piper. What's your name?"

"Jason Grace." He said quietly, knowing it would mean nothing to her.

"Jason? Like… like the Greek hero?" Leo asked, fidgeting with a lugnut.

Jason smiled, remembering the words. *The Sea Monster of Troy shall be slain by the pride of Juno.*

The blood. The bodies. Connor losing his sight. Thomas his hearing. Summoning lightning. So much hurt. So much suffering. That was Rome. Failing forward.

*Maybe I don't want to be a Roman. Maybe I want to try being a Greek.*

"Yes. Let's go with that."

Piper noticed marks on his arms. Ten of them.

"S.P.Q.R. Ten marks. Is that… like a tattoo, or something?" She asked.

Ten years serving the Legion. Senatus Populusque Romanus. Jason was done with the Senate, with Octavian, with the politics of it all. Bryce abusing his power. Reyna on trial for treason. So many times the Legion had exposed the rot and done nothing. He had done nothing. Not when it mattered.

"Our boy is part of a gang!" Leo exclaimed, grinning from ear to ear. "A Latin-themed gang! So do you just have meetings about who will be Praetor and going to war and stuff, or is it like an actual thing?"

Standing in the Senate. Leading them into the bloodbath they would call the Battle of Mount Othrys, the Charge of Mount Tam. Him leading Othrys. Reyna leading Tam. So much loss, blood, pain.

"Not a gang. A bit more… rigid." He said.

His vision blurred as his back pressed into the broken-down bus.

"I mean, unless you just had a really cruel Latin teacher or something." Piper muttered.

Lupa's face came to mind. *This is the way of the Legion, Jason Grace. Live, or die. Adapt, or be left behind. A wolf has no weakness, no fear, no mercy.*

"You could put it that way." Jason said.

"Don't worry, Jason. We'll get you to Coach Hedge. He'll know what to do." Leo promised.

"I trust you." Jason said, before he lost consciousness again.

As his mind faded to dark, he only thought one thing.

*Juno, please, just this once. Let me forget what I have lost.*
