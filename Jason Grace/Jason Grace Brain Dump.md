# Jason Grace Brain Dump
Reyna approached from behind him.
"You have served the Legion ten years now, Jason." She said amicably.
Jason stiffened just a fraction.
"Let me guess," he said, so tired of the man's antics after seven years that he was actually using snark, "Octavian insists I resign as Praetor."
Reyna nodded bitterly.
Octavian was powerful in the Senate, and very pursuasive. He feared that if he were to be absent, to not be here to keep the Augery in check, the man might pick up his namesake, use it as a weapon, and control the Senate completely.
"He argues that you have done your service to Rome, no one is forcing you to stay here forever. Retire, take your place in New Rome." She said, patting him on the back. "Do yourself a favor and fight for yourself for once instead of incessantly backing the underdog."
Jason gave a long, bitter sigh.
"And who does Octavian recommend take my place? That I nominate for my position?" He asked, though he already knew the answer.
"Luke Barks." She answered.
Octavian's glorified lapdog, practically in the name.
But when Jason thought of who might replace him as Praetor of the Twelfth Legion, Barks was nowhere near the top of the list. Barks wasn't even on the list.
At the top of the list, for the past two years, had been the same name.
Frank Zhang.
"Tell Octavian I will consider his feedback in my decision." He said. "But that as long as I am Praetor, that is all he will ever get from me. A voice in my head to consider."
For the past two years, Frank had come to the monthly games to take the big fortress, knowing he was doomed to fail, that the Fifth Cohort never won, and fought anyway. Tried anyway. Did his best.
It was a form of honor that Octavian would never understand. One that Jason had spent years trying to emulate. Knowing you were fighting a losing battle, and giving it your all anyway, because it was your duty, and it was the right thing to do.

"Frank Zhang, don't underestimate yourself. Very few men, few Roman in fact, can fight with the Fifth Cohort, month after month, knowing they are going to lose, and still give it their all because it's the right thing to do." Jason said, patting Zhang on the back.

"Zhang, on my honor as Praetor of New Rome, I believe you will do great things for the Legion. Very few people can match your spirit, and even fewer can match it for very long. I believe that one day, maybe not soon, maybe not for a long time, but one day, you will be called the Hero of the Twelfth Legion because you choose to do the right thing over the easy thing every single time. I dare say, Frank, that they will call you a one of the Heroes of Olympus." Jason said.

"You don't know how powerful I am, I don't know how powerful I am. So let's find out. A titan, or a son of Jupiter who, just this once, has decided to stop holding back?" Jason asked.

"I feel like I live in a world made of cardboard. Always taking constant care not to break something," Jason said, then cracked the pillar with his bare fist, "to break someone. Never allowing myself to lose control, even for a moment, or someone could die."
Jason eyed the bodies of his fallen comrades. The Legion was overwhelmed, still dealing with the monsters outside, it didn't have the manpower to bury this many dead.
"But look around you, Krios. There's no one around for me to hurt." Jason eyed, then pointed at the crippled fortress. "This building will be rubble in minutes. You're a titan, you can take it."
Jason, after 12 years of service to the Roman Legion, finally allowed the lightning within him to run wild. It buzzed at his fingertips, dipping and twisting back and forth across his wrists, then running circles across his muscular frame.
"What we have here is a rare opportunity for me to cut loose—to stop worrying about collateral damage—and show you just how powerful I really am. Because I don't know either."
What followed was a display of power so intense that Krios was nearly blown away. Winds surged into the throne room as lightning gathered in the roof and Jason's hands.
"Twenty. Forty. Sixty. Eighty." Jason chanted, willing the winds to rise to match his ferocity, and they did. 20 mph. 40. 60. Then 80, until Jason knew he should be having a hard time standing up as air sent swords, shields and spears flying in every direction, but instead he felt completely fine.
Jason had never felt so relaxed in his life.
"Hundred. Hundred fifty. Two hundred." Jason kept going, until the wind pattern set off the weather probes and the local tornado sirens began going off.
The pressure should've burst Jason's lungs, but Jason wasn't fazed by it.
Krios was scared.
Letting his palms connect with a sound like cracking thunder, Jason allowed the winds to reach 300 mph on either side of him. Krios had to rely on his titan power just to remain standing.
"Do you see it, Krios? I'm a living, breathing hurricane. When I get angry, levees break. And now, I'm going to show you why hurricanes always strike in the West. Because I am livid, and not even I know how strong I really am when I really, truly try…to kill someone."
