The two bronze wolves, Aurum and Argentum, ran over the Pomerian Line just like they'd done days ago, and lunged for the kill.

The daughter of Bellona sheathed her gladius, and held their gaze.

"HEEL!" She shouted.

Fifty feet.

Forty feet.

Twenty.

Ten.

Only when the dogs were about to make contact did her voice break through, and both stopped in their tracks, slowly resting on their back paws.

Gwen stepped forward.

"Reyna Avilla Ramirez-Arellano. Probatio of the Fifth Cohort. For this act of valor, I grant you the rank of Legionnaire."

Reyna stepped forward.

For about five seconds, Jason was fairly sure that Reyna would tell Gwen to shove her rank of Legionnaire where the eagle don't bite.

Then she nodded her head.

"Thank you, Gw- Praetor Gwen," Reyna corrected herself, attempting to sound resolute and calm as she eyed Jason with a smile, "I accept this rank with gratitude and thank you for your great generosity."

Jason finally realized it. Reyna was attempting to impress him… by doing her best impression of him. Imitation was the sincerest form of flattery.
