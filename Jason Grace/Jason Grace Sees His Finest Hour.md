He had never imagined it would end like this. Breathing his last here, in a cave, surrounded by manias and ghouls and Reyna. Reyna's first major quest as a Legonnaire. The one time she had told Aurum and Argentum to stay behind.

They both had regretted that hours ago.

Now it was just a Son of Jupiter, a Legionnaire of the Third Cohort, a gold coin, a bronze sword, and two bronze sabers. Against a horde of terrifying demons from their pasts and deepest fears… that could only be killed by the one metal they didn't have. Imperial Gold.

The Son of Jupiter stopped questioning the statement.

*We are going to die here.*

Reyna looked at him, scared and confused. Her hair was a total mess, and neither of them cared. She knew, he knew, and this was it.

"It's pronounced Ra-mee-rez Ah-ray-ah-no." She said.

Jason was about to question why she was telling him this if that really was the name of a little girl in San Juan and she's really left it in Puerto Rico, then he realized.

*She's saying it because it no longer matters.*

Reyna and Jason sat on the ground together and prepared for their finest hour. Her eyes bored into his. His into hers. She shook her head like she couldn't believe what she was about to do.

"It has been an honor serving at your side, Prae—" He tried to say, expecting her to protest, expecting her to say that she wasn't a Praetor, or that he could stick his rank where the eagle didn't bite, or any other host of things.

Instead, Reyna kissed him.

His eyes widened immediately, then closed again. He'd never thought about her that way before, but if this was how she wanted to die, then he wasn't going to stop her. His arms around her neck, hers on his shoulders, steady and calm and fierce. It tasted like Reyna Avilla Ramirez-Arellano. It tasted like a girl who laid her heart out bare because if she was going to die, here and now, it wasn't going to be with regrets.

Two minutes later, she broke it off, panting and sweating. She didn't dare look at him, didn't dare talk. It had happened. It was over. No words were spoke between them, they just stared into the horde as it surrounded them.

He raised his blade. She raised hers.

"Together." They said at the same time, like the Fifth Cohort had taught them. "For better, for worse."

And they charged the horde, knowing their task was impossible, that they were doomed to die here, because as Reyna Ramirez-Arellano had taught him and Jason Grace had taught her…

…they were both the kind of person to go down swinging.

Jason sliced at ghouls and manias until his fingers ached. After that, he abandoned his sword and punched the armor off of them until his knuckles bled. After that, he and Reyna laid on the ground together, covered in blood and sweat and tears. Smiling together. Jason picked up the coin in his right pocket, the golden coin Lupa had given him a year ago.

"Lupa told me… " He wheezed with half his ribcage screaming at him, "… that you don't know until you act."

Reyna looked at him, so many wounds decorating her armor and face that it was hard to see where the red fabric began and the blood ended.

"The coin. What's it do?" She whispered.

"I've been terrified to use it. I'm a Son of Jupiter. I'm strong. Too strong. If I can't control it, we both could die."

Reyna punched him in the shoulder.

"We're both going to die anyway." She muttered. "How does it work?"

"I… don't know." He admitted.

"It's a coin, Jason. Try flipping it." She said, laughing, because what else could anyone do besides laugh?

Jason picked the coin out of his right pocket, and flipped it. No matter what the weapon did, their situation literally could not get any worse.

It landed on his chest. Not as a coin. But as an Imperial Gold gladius furnished with the decorations of the Twelfth Legion.

Imperial Gold.

They both couldn't help it. They just burst out laughing and laughed until they pretty soon started crying from their own laughter. The manias looked at them. The ghouls looked at them. In the face of total fear, of imminent death, two teenagers were laughing hysterically. No…

…maniacally.

Faced with such an absurd lack of fear, the ghouls began to burn and flee, at first individually, then in waves. The manias looked at the one metal that could kill them and backed away. Realizing that he was standing next to the one person in the whole Legion that could take him at full throttle, Jason drew the blade, feeling its lightning spark…

…feeling the lightning mending broken ribs and shattered wrists and bloodied fingers. He stood to his full height. And Jason laughed like a maniac.

"I'm *so* sorry, did I offend you? Me and my girlfriend here were just cracking up at how funny you guys look with your heads all turned."

*Girlfriend.*

Reyna blushed, but said nothing. With Jason's back turned to her as he faced the horde, hacking apart and burning up manias left and right in a tirade of hysterical laughter and fury, she clenched her fist and mouthed one word.

*Score.*

…

After they left, they noticed the leopard had been shot.

"I have a feeling that Bacchus doesn't want his leopard back anymore." Reyna smirked.

"Agreed." Jason sighed. "What do we do with it?"

She laughed, eyeing the abusive father that had once made her dread her father, but after being faced with literal imminent death, and after kissing her crush, none of that really fazed her anymore. The man sat on his front porch, shotgun out, looking at the dead leopard.

"It's my lawn, and I will decide what I do with it!" He shouted angrily.

Reyna looked at Jason.

"How about… we sell it?" She asked, and he realized where she was going with this. The leopard was Bacchus' sacred animal. This poor schmuck had just shot it. Maybe to get some real justice in the Legion, you had to get a bit creative.

He nodded.

"Yeah. How about a yard sale?"
