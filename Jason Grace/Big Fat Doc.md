## Jason Grace

**Identity & Background**
Jason Grace is a **Roman demigod**, son of Jupiter and mortal Beryl Grace, and younger brother of Thalia Grace ([Riordan Wiki][1], [Wikipedia][2]).

**Abilities & Role**
He inherits Roman powers from Jupiter: weather control, lightning summoning, wind manipulation, storm spirit summoning, and flying ([Wikipedia][2]). Praetor by age fifteen, he led the Twelfth Legion at Camp Jupiter and served as head counselor of Cabin One at Camp Half-Blood ([Riordan Wiki][1], [Wikipedia][2]). Later he became **Pontifex Maximus**, bridging Greek and Roman traditions by building shrines and traveling between camps ([Wikipedia][2]).

**Story Highlights**

* Introduced in *The Lost Hero*: suffers from amnesia, later remembers being a son of Jupiter and powers manifesting ([Wikipedia][3]).
* In *The Heroes of Olympus*: reunited with Thalia, starts relationship with Piper McLean ([Wikipedia][2]).
* In *The House of Hades*: gives up praetorship to Frank Zhang to embrace Greek identity ([Wikipedia][4]).
* In *The Burning Maze* (Trials of Apollo series): sacrifices himself to save Piper, Apollo, and Meg; killed by Caligula ([Wikipedia][2]). Avenged and honored with a state funeral; Nico senses he’s moved on to somewhere better—Elysium or a potential rebirth ([Riordan Wiki][1], [Wikipedia][2]).

## 2. Percy Jackson

**Identity & Background**
Perseus “Percy” Jackson is a **Greek demigod**, son of Poseidon and mortal Sally Jackson ([Riordan Wiki][5], [Wikipedia][6]). Born August 18, appeared as a twelve-year-old in *The Lightning Thief* ([Wikipedia][6]).

**Personality & Abilities**
Diagnosed with ADHD and dyslexia—cast as a liability, but Riordan frames them as defying nature, where dyslexia is a battlefield-reflex code for reading Ancient Greek and ADHD keeps him alert in combat ([Riordan Wiki][5], [Wikipedia][6]). Personality is “changeable like the sea,” unpredictable yet abiding loyalty (his fatal flaw) ([Wikipedia][6]). Powers include water control, hurricane creation, underwater breathing, talking to horses and fish; a skilled swordfighter and leader ([Wikipedia][6]).

**Role Over the Series**

* Head Counselor of Poseidon’s Cabin at Camp Half-Blood; later praetor of the Twelfth Legion at Camp Jupiter ([Riordan Wiki][5], [Wikipedia][6]).
* Appears throughout *Percy Jackson & the Olympians*, *Heroes of Olympus*, *Trials of Apollo*, etc. ([Riordan Wiki][5]).
* After *The Last Olympian*, he loses memory in *The Son of Neptune*, struggles, becomes praetor again ([Wikipedia][6]).
* Later, moral complexity: tortures Akhlys (water in body) in *The House of Hades* until Annabeth pulls him back; fear of drowning, less confidence in fighting, ambiguous ethics ([Wikipedia][6]).
* Plans with Annabeth to finish school and college in New Rome; accepted on scholarship but passes on a quest to focus on future ([Wikipedia][6]).

## 3. *The Lightning Thief* (Book 1)

**Overview**
Published July 1, 2005 (hardcover), first in the *Percy Jackson & the Olympians* series ([Wikipedia][7]). Introduces Percy discovering the demigod world, quest to prevent war among gods ([Riordan Wiki][8], [Wikipedia][9]).

**Plot Summary**

* Percy, a troubled twelve-year-old, diagnosed ADHD/dyslexia, is expelled from school again. On a field trip, his water powers accidentally drench a bully; his teacher turns into a Fury and attacks ([Riordan Wiki][8]).
* Mother disappears; Percy is brought to Camp Half-Blood, learns he's Poseidon’s son, meets Grover (satyr friend) and Annabeth ([Wikipedia][9], [Riordan Wiki][8]).
* Zeus suspects Percy of stealing his lightning bolt; to clear his name, Percy sets out on a quest west to the Underworld to confront Hades, retrieve the bolt, and rescue his mom (whose soul Hades may have). Learns it’s a framed mission, must prevent war ([Wikipedia][9], [Riordan Wiki][8]).

**Themes & Characters**

* Themes: identity, loyalty, heroism, confronting destiny.
* Key characters introduced: Percy Jackson, Annabeth Chase, Grover Underwood, gods (Poseidon, Zeus, Hades), various mythological creatures based on Riordan Wiki listing ([Riordan Wiki][8]).
