The Son of Jupiter, forbidden from crossing into the domain of Neptune from birth under penalty of drowning, eyed the mighty Pacific and its waves with dread. He didn't know if what he was doing was right, or if he was risking his life for nothing, or if Neptune would simply kill him to prove a point about how even children of the eldest gods needed to stay in their lane.

Jason hesitated, gaze shifting to the little dingy like it might spontaneously combust. There was a reason that New Rome had no navy.

Testing Neptune's patience was never a good idea.

Gwen nudged him forward anyway.

"Go. Either it is on the boat, or it is not. You will not know until you get there." She said, armor gleaming in the late evening light. There was no wind to catch her cropped hair. She did not need it. She had the authority of living lightning. The wind would know its place soon enough.

He could really use some lightning right now.

"If it really is Octavian holding it," He said quietly, brushing the bronze sword he'd been issued, "then what will I even do? Arrest him?"

He didn't even have a sword to call his own. That was how alone he was now. Electricity had left his fingertips behind. No demigod powers to call upon now. Maybe that was why the hair on the back of his neck was standing at attention even when his head hung low.

"You stand at the base of the mountain and worry about the summit. Don't. Act, Jason. Decide, be wrong, fail. Learn, adapt, overcome. Move on." She said, pushing him slowly into the small dingy they'd built together.

"You guys done yet?" Dakota shouted from the passenger side window of the truck, a bottle of wine half-finished in his hand, not even sounding tipsy. That was Bacchus for you.

Jason stepped into the boat. He put his legs over the side and lowered himself onto one of the wooden benches. It rocked back and forth violently beneath his weight. His back straightened. Maybe he'd gone too far, pushed his luck.

The vessel leveled out, and steadied itself.

"No, Son of Bacchus, we are not 'done yet'. Return to your mild alcoholism. We'll be certain to inform you when we're done saving the world." Gwen shouted back without her voice varying. Under her breath, Jason thought she muttered 'from men like you', but he didn't comment on it.

Trying to calm his raging nerves about being *at sea* in *Neptune's domain*, he asked a question.

"Do you think… I would like your job? Being Praetor? Working with Samuel?"

Gwen smiled at him, and pushed the boat off the shore, watching the distance between them grow steadily.

"My job? You would despise it. Your calls would determine whether people live or die, and you struggle even now with what to eat for breakfast."

Jason Grace. Praetor. She was right. If he had to be the one telling people what to do, he wouldn't like that at all.

"Yet grow a few inches, Grace, and you may find yourself becoming a Praetor for the same reason I did." She said, and they said it at the same time because she'd told the story so many times before.

He looked at the huge ship just beyond the shoreline where Octavian might or might not be hiding the First Cohort's Eagle. The Crown Jewel. Seventy feet long, twenty feet wide, with nobody aboard. The ship didn't even seem to have anyone manning it. He tightened the ropes of the dingy to give his hands something to do besides writhe, and his lips moved in-tune with Gwen's.

"Because it must be done."
