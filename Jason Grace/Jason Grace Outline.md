# Outline

## Eagle Thief

### Act One

- For the first time in Jason's life, he finds himself without his powers.
- Lupa tells him demigods have always been of two camps, two sides, which Jason takes as a nod to how he's been split between two sides of himself for years now. She explains that something has gone missing on the other side, and since what rises in the East always settles in the West, the Legion may lose something of similar importance soon.
- The signs line up too well for Jason to ignore, yet he fears speaking up will be paramount to treason against Rome: A fate that his own reputation could survive but that the Fifth Cohort's wouldn't.
