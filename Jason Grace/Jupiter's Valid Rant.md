A lot of things had just happened. The dead were still being gathered. The Greeks, and the Romans. Jupiter had risked his kingdom for his son, and gotten both. Now Romans and Greeks stood at odds, but not demigods. Gods.

Hera and Juno eyed each other with visible disgust. Ares, of course, was grinding a spear between his teeth in what Mars had remarked looked oddly like a toddler eating a crayon. Hermes hadn't even bothered to show up, and Jupiter had said nothing, because after nearly losing Jason himself, and Zeus nearly losing Thalia, nobody was going to be the one to scold the god who had just lost his son. Diana and Artemis stood with their respective Hunters, a look of mutual respect.

"In hindsight, I'd say this went remarkably well." Zeus said.

On any other day, Jupiter would've held his tongue. Restrained himself. The sea did not like being restrained, but the sky had to be restrained constantly. But Olympus was still burning. Manhattan was still burning. Hell, parts of Rome were still burning. So just like his son had done a few hours ago, Jupiter stopped holding back.

"Which part? The part where two hundred good men and women died today because you were too high and mighty to do your own children the bare minimum, or the part where you would've burned down two kingdoms on the altar of your own ego?" Jupiter growled. "I get confused when I somehow meet a man who makes _Octavian_ look selfless."

Zeus blanched, but the Roman god of the Sky wasn't done yet.

"I believe my recent spike in skepticism began when you sent a Fury after a 12 year old boy with no plan." He growled. Zeus interjected.

"I believed he had stolen my Master Bolt!"

The Greek god was started to get heated.

_Good._ Jupiter thought. _He deserves to have someone finally step up to him._

"And what was the Fury meant to do exactly? Oh, sorry, Lord Zeus, I stole your Master Bolt, mea maxima culpa, I'll get it right back to you? Either you send a Fury after an innocent 12 year old, or you put a Fury toe to toe with your own Master Bolt and pray the kid who is arrogant enough to steal it isn't arrogant enough to also take a shot as said Fury with it!" Jupiter sputtered. "It's so stupid! And reckless! And impulsive! An act of pure ambition and ego from a king who wants nothing more than to start a war and get back to the glory days! Are you happy having gotten your war? Are you satisfied that BOTH our kingdoms burn because you couldn't bear the thought of being wrong about anything ever?"
