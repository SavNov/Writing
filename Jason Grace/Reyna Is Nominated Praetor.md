Jason knew even before the trial began that this was going to be hard to watch.

Reyna was a good Legionnaire, and a good servant of the Third Cohort, but she'd still tried to murder Bryce Lawrence, still refused to back down. A quality that on any other day would have her be honored, but now it might be the thing to get her killed.

Senators were crammed into their seats, togas fitting on their bodies elegantly as row after row of the Senate Floor filled with powerful families, powerful people, powerful heritages. Octavian wasn't the scrawny little boy Jason had once asked for a prophecy two years ago, he was an intelligent and ambitious statesman. The Augury. Dressed in a toga, watching carefully, like a wolf ready to assert itself as lead of the pack… by killing the Alpha Wolf in cold blood.

Reyna was on trial for treason, and though Octavian was the picture of sorrow, Jason knew the boy was enjoying this more than he would ever admit.

Praetor Gwen caught his eye. She mouthed to him.

*I know. It hurts us both. Yet, just as I yearn to help, Reyna must fight her own battles here. For good, for ill.*

A gavel banged. Reyna sat in the center of the Senate, on the floor, in proper military dress, a Legionnaire to the bitter end. Doors opened at the end of the hall, and faces turned to look.

Bryce's body, wounded and bloody as his toga leaked onto the marble floor, arrived on a stretcher. Bryce groaned. It was a performance and both Jason and Reyna knew it. Her teeth ground, but she said nothing. Gasps all around.

"Former Legionnaire, how are your wounds?" Octavian asked, smooth as butter as he guided their attention over.

Bryce just grunted and smiled weakly.

"I've been better."

Senator Octavius, Octavian's father, rose from his seat.

"I must confess, Legionnaire of the Third, I had not been informed of the true extent of your wounds." The man said, voice quiet. The familiar blonde hair and brown eyes bored into Bryce's, then Reyna's, then Jason's. "If I had known the true severity, I would have never asked you to be present at this hearing."

A few lahrs grumbled to themselves. "She must pay for her actions today."

Then Gaius showed up. This was bad.

"Let us get this trial moving so Bryce can get to healing and Reyna can get to paying."

Jason shivered. The man's voice was ghastly, like Death's vocal chords were being borrowed and the words were being pushed through them despite the rotting flesh's protests.

Consul Gaius circled the room as a man on a mission.

"Reyna Avilla Ramirez-Arellano. Legionnaire of the Third Cohort. You stand accused of treason against New Rome for the attempted murder of former Legionnaire Bryce Lawrence. How do you plead to these accusations?" He said.

Reyna gulped softly, then met the Consul's eyes with the resolve of a woman who knew she was going to die and had come to terms with that.

"Guilty. I did what I had to do. He evaded justice. Powerful people with powerful names exploited our Senate, my Senate, and allowed the Legionnaire who murdered his own Centurion to walk away with a slap on the wrist. I was… attempting to… correct that error." Reyna declared.

Jason and Gwen's eyes locked. Praetor Samuel seemed shocked… and proud, almost. Even Consul Gaius seemed stunned.

"You… you are aware that the punishment for your crime is execution, correct? That is the punishment for treason." Gaius asked, like he couldn't believe the words coming out of Reyna's mouth.

Her response had enough steel to hold up the foundations of the Senate Floor all on its own.

"Lupa once taught me that to die in service of the Legion was honor. To give my life as an offering to the soul of Rome was the greatest act of valor a Roman could achieve. If I must die for my service to the Legion, then I die willingly and with honor."

You could have heard a pin drop in the silence that followed. Togas ruffled in the wind. All of them staring at Reyna in sheer disbelief.

"You make no move to defend yourself, daughter of Bellona?" Gaius whispered, unable to believe the words reaching his ears.

Reyna smirked.

"Consul, if I actually defended myself here, in the Senate, you'd be wiping the blood off your togas for weeks."

Consul Gaius Octavius was rattled so badly he sat down again. "No weakness, no fear, no mercy."

Every pair of eyes in the Senate widened as that phrase clicked. He hadn't even realized what he'd been saying, it had been noting more than a passing observation.

Praetor Gwendolyn had never looked so proud.

She stood up, walked to the middle of the Senate floor like she had grown past caring about tradition and legal codes, and began to speak.

"Senators, your ears hear but do not understand. Your eyes see but do not acknowledge. Look before you."

Now it was Gwen's turn to walk in circles like she knew something they didn't.

"Our entire lives, we have heard the stories. Warriors taking command in the most dire of moments. Bloodbaths that turn Probatios into Centurions overnight. We took a vow that all those who honor the teachings and ways of Rome, of Lupa, of the gods, and that of Jupiter, would have a place here. A home. No matter where they come from, what their name is, what weapon they fight with, all those willingly to die in service of Rome can stand proud under the banner of the Twelfth Legion Fulminata."

She nudged Reyna.

"Lupa has taught us since we were children, either in person, or through those she has mentored, that the way of the wolf was how we would know our leaders. By their actions. By their command. By those who show no weakness, show no fear," She said, only for a Senator in the back row to finish the sentence like a kid reciting a rhyme.

", show no mercy."

She nodded, smiling with pride.

"The battle is before you, children of Rome. First blood has been spilled. Look around you. Who is the one the wolf spoke of, among us? The one who shows no weakness, no fear, no mercy? Who kills, as Romulus once did, because it must be done? Lay down your pride. Pick up the pride of the Legion. Your New Rome rusts and decays, it's Legionnaires betray and deceive, nostalgia lies to us as we try to cling to tradition even as those who first wrote the laws demand we evolve. This is the way of the Twelfth Legion, Senators. Live, or die. Sink, or swim. Adapt, or be culled by those who do. If you do not like this, then New Rome has no place for you, and no interest in your service. You may go. Do not come back."

Five seconds later, every single person in the entire Senate clapped emphatically.

That was Praetor Gwendolyn. Authority like living lightning.

"What do you recommend the Senate take in terms of action regarding Reyna, Praetor?" Gaius asked.

"None at all. I acquit Reyna of all charges. In fact, I believe I have finally found the successor Lupa taught me to look for." Gwen said.

Grumbling ensued. "What are you implying, Praetor Gwendolyn?"

Gwen just smiled.

"I nominate Legionnaire Reyna Avilla Ramirez-Arellano to the rank of Praetor of the Twelfth Legion. She will grow to be the Praetor the Legion needs in time."

Reyna was so flabbergasted she just stuttered in coherently for a few seconds.

"But that… that's YOUR rank! I can't just— there can't be THREE Praetors of the Legion! That's not how this works!"

Jason smiled this time, realizing what Gwen was intending to do.

"I demote myself honorably to the rank of Centurion of the Fifth Cohort, on the condition that Reyna take my place." She said.

More gasping and grumbling.

"That's Centurion Dakota's rank!" A Senator in the back insisted.

"Yes, it is, and Centurion Dakota is… Dakota." She retorted.

No one really challenged that.

"You will demote him to Legionnaire, then." Gaius realized.

Gwen nodded.

"Consider it an intervention. A wake-up call that in order to be worthy of the post, he must prove he is worthy of it."
