Jason honestly did not know why he was here. At least, not here specifically. At a tavern/bar on the West Coast near the Bay Area. He told himself that after Aeolus had been a dead-end, the winds had been no help, and he still didn't know where that Trojan Sea Monster was, he needed a break. Take a page out of Dakota's book. Cut loose, relax, dare to enjoy himself.

The electricity sparking in his veins as he sat down at a table disagreed with him though. He *was* here for a reason. He just didn't know what that reason was yet.

Sports playoffs played on a dozen flat screen TVs. The radio in the ceiling played the only song that suited the place:  California, knows how to party  .

Men and women in every form of dress known to humankind gathered at tables and drank, and drank, and drank some more.

He got stares from a couple of girls, who definitely weren't unhappy to get a better look at him. Near the back, he recognized one that seemed to be staring at him just a bit too intensely. Like she knew something. She walked over and sat next to him at his table. Her legs wobbled as she steadied herself on the armrest.

*She's drunk.* Jason thought.

Then he remembered Dakota. Dakota on a bad day was drunk. The girl's cheeks were plastic bucket red and she was struggling to even stay in the chair.

"My name… " She said, then slumped over onto his shoulder. He respectfully pushed her back into her personal space. "…is Reyna. Reyna Ramirez-Arellano. But you… well you can call me anything you like."

*Correction: Reyna Ramirez-Arellano is hammered.*

"Hello, Reyna." Jason said, trying to be respectful *and* not give her any excuse to get closer. "Where are you from?"

The question seemed to make those dilated-to-kingdom-come eyes narrow just a tiny bit.

"Puerto Rico." She said, like it was the only two words of her past she still remembered in this state. "And there was something about… a salon, or something… hard to remember. I worked for a witch, I know that much."

Jason had thought she'd said something a bit more colorful for a second, then reconsidered. A witch. The Sea of Monsters.

If there was a chance she wasn't just using a figure of speech, maybe this wasn't a complete dead-end.

"A witch?"

She nodded and rolled her eyes like this was old news. "Yeah, a witch. All magic and everything. I did people's hair. A blonde, and there was a boy, too. I think his name was…"

"Focus." Jason said calmly, steadying her with a hand on her shoulder. "Tell me about the witch. What was her name?"

Reyna recoiled at the sensation instantly, and Jason responded by releasing her.

"Don't touch me." She growled.

He threw his hands up.

"My apologies. I won't do it again." He assured her.

"Circe. I'm starting to remember now, thanks to you. I was happy. Me and my sister protected each other. And then that… boy… turned Blackbeard and his crew into people again. And they used us. In terrible ways." Reyna said, dropping to a whisper. "Thank you, boy, I had nearly managed to forget that before you brought it up."

He sighed, staring down at the floor. Reyna was starting to lose it again, swaying back and forth in her seat.

"What was I talking about again? It was something about pirates. I don't like pirates. Don't know why, just never have. But you on the other hand… you I think I could go a couple rounds with."

Jason prayed on every god in Rome that she was referring to sparring.

"Anyway… who is your mother?" He asked.

She studied him for a long moment, fading in and out of her drunken stupor.

"Bellona. My mother is Bellona. It doesn't make sense, but it's true."

His eyes widened just as hers had. A Roman demigod? Out here?

"Are you listening to me, Reyna?" He asked, locking eyes with her to keep her focused on him and him alone.

She nodded like she was dreaming.

"Find Lupa. Loo-pah. Wolf goddess. North of here. She'll train you. Show you what to do. Can you remember that for me?" He said firmly.

"Not your scene, eh? Alright then. If I go to this 'wolf goddess'" Reyna said, putting the last two words in heavy quotes, "will I see your face again?"

Jason nodded reluctantly. "You will. I'll be waiting for you."

He stood up from his seat, now knowing at least where he needed to be headed towards, and Reyna stood up to follow him.

"I'll come with—" She said, then stumbled and struggled to get her bearings.

"You're drunk, Reyna. Get some rest. You have a long journey ahead of you."

"I'm not—" Reyna spat, only to be cut off as she tipped over like a ship and capsized into the table, out like a light.

"—drunk." He finished her sentence for her. He figured he could get away with one tiny quip. "For some strange reason, Reyna, I doubt that highly."
