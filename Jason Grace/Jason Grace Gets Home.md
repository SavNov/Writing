"Understood, Centurion Jason. However, there is someone I believe you need to meet." Gwen said.

A woman suddenly burst through the Senate doors, a very angry chunk of Terminus shouting her down from behind, in a raggedy t-shirt and jeans like the concept of a dress code offended her. To her left and right, blades hung at her side. Curved. She smiled at him, then looked apologetic.

Her mouth opened and closed for a second, but then she settled herself.

"You need to learn the Roman way." Gwen added, calm yet stern.

Reyna sputtered in protest. "If your way is *so* much better, then beat my way with yours! I'm not just going to train in a style without knowing its better than what I already have."

Two Praetors, two Centurions, and ten Legionnaire's sighed.

Reyna was going to be a project that only Jason Grace, Son of Jupiter, had the restraint and patience to handle. Or, more accurately, not kill in less than ten minutes for her blatant disrespect.

"If this is what being Centurion is like," Jason groaned, "remind to *never* become Praetor."
