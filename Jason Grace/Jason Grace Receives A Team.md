Jason had finally done it. A team. A quest. A monster. The Sea Monster of Troy would be slain by the pride of Juno. His name was Jason.

Who the Pluto else was it going to be?

And there was another prophecy, too. One he assumed was about him, given he'd been raised by wolves and had dueled Octavian for the First Cohort's Eagle last year.

The Legion shall find its leader in the wolf who has no weakness, no fear, no mercy.

It might as well be a giant blinking arrow pointing at him.

Yes, he was the Son of Jupiter, and this was a sea journey. But he'd already been across the sea once, to duel Octavian, and that had been scary, but it had turned out fine. Clearly, when he'd promised Neptune that in a war among the gods, Neptune would not be the winner, that had been enough to convince the god of the sea to think twice about sinking his ship.

This was going to go fine.

A bunch of Probatios from the Fifth Cohort stood at attention, a boat behind them on the beach that definitely had not been there before.

A forty-foot sail boat with masts and rigging all ready-to-go with harpoons and chains waiting to be used. A fine vessel, for a fine demigod and a fine quest.
